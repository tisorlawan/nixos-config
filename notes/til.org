- [https://igupta.in/blog/what-i-learned-being-a-programmer/]

*** Namespace: an isolation primitive that helps us to isolate various types of resources e.g. network
- Network namespace
- Mount
- UTS or Hostname namespace
- PID namespace
- Inter process communication or IPC namespace
- cgroup namespace
- User namespace
*** Cgroups: controlling the amount of the resources that a process can consume
*** Capabilities: limits the use of root privileges
*** Seccomp: It limits the use of syscalls
